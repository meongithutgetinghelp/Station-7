# The Passenger — current gameplay rules

## Nights 1–3: observation

The entity appears only as a passenger trying to board. It cannot attack, kill or abduct anyone. Compare tickets and IDs, investigate ownership at the booth computer, and compare the visible passenger with CCTV. An impersonator has an unverified identity and is absent from the camera image; a genuine person carrying a stolen ticket has a confirmed identity with an ownership mismatch.

Deny disguised intruders. Ordinary document errors also require denial. Clearing all inspections without admitting an intruder secures the early-night threat objective. Disturbances build gradually: Night 1 has no automatic entity sabotage, Night 2 has a slower disturbance interval, and Night 3 resumes the existing sabotage sequence; the station starts dark; staff switch on mains before signing in.

## Nights 4–7: escalation

A separate hostile form can stalk, hear footsteps and doors, pursue visible people, sabotage infrastructure and threaten passengers. It wakes earlier and becomes faster on later nights. Walls and closed doors interrupt sight; after losing sight for eight seconds it returns to stalking. Crouch inside a closed restroom stall to hide.

The flashlight briefly drives back an unidentified intruder. Collect the booth, Security and Electrical reports and confirm identification at Security. Then hold a direct beam on it for about 2.5 seconds to banish it. Charge the flashlight or use a spare battery when needed.

Threatened passengers freeze with a danger marker. Interact and lead them into Security or the booth before their timer expires. Night 4 allows one loss; Nights 5–7 allow none. Early nights never create these danger/abduction states. Contact during pursuit ends the player's run and offers retry/load; open work panels do not make the player invulnerable.

## Securing a shift

Sign in, patrol the platform, complete the shift’s ticket decisions (9 on fresh Night 1, 24 on later nights) with at least 66% accuracy, board passengers, keep cleanliness at least 70%, complete outstanding repairs and contain the threat according to the night's rules. Once secured, the remaining quiet hours advance to the dawn report.

The original procedural characters and simple animations remain subject to graphical and balance review. Automated checks cover state transitions, physical routes, containment and safety rules; they do not establish scare quality or difficulty balance.

## Optional pacing layer

A separate camera-only figure can appear on CAM 03 during Night 3. It is not a ticket passenger and cannot affect verdicts or required evidence. On Night 4, a once-per-shift departure aftermath precedes the first automatic hostile wake; the figure has no collision/attacks and does not block boarding. See builds/pacing-qa/REPORT.md.
